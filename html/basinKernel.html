shx.basinKernel

Band-limited basin kernels from polygons, masks, or functions — with great-circle buffering and spectral tapering.

Syntax

[b, info] = shx.basinKernel(idx, region)
[b, info] = shx.basinKernel(idx, region, BufferKm=300, TaperKm=350)

Description

Builds the spherical-harmonic coefficient vector of the (band-limited) indicator function of a region by exact Gauss–Legendre quadrature, b = Y' * (w .* mask), in the ordering of idx = shx.shIndex(...). The result feeds ts.basinAverage(b) and shx.basinDeconvolve.

Inputs

NameType / sizeDescription
idxstructfrom shx.shIndex
regionhandle | Kx2 double | Ngrid x 1vectorized f(latDeg,lonDeg) in [0,1]; polygon vertices [lat lon]; or a mask on the quadrature grid
BufferKm(1,1) double, 0>0 grows / <0 shrinks the region by exact great-circle distance before quadrature
TaperKm(1,1) double, 0spectral Jekeli-Gaussian taper of the kernel (soft edges, less ringing/leakage)
R(1,1) double, 6378136.3sphere radius [m] for km conversions (default, overridable)

Outputs

NameType / sizeDescription
b(P,1) doublekernel coefficients, idx ordering
infostructareaFraction, nGridPoints, buffered, tapered
Best practice: match TaperKm to the smoothing radius of your coefficient filter; use BufferKm to trade leakage-in against leakage-out. Theory and worked examples: workflow guide, “Basin averages”.
