Comparison suite (v2.6.0)

v2.6.0 Standard metrics for comparing solutions and series in the spectral, spatial and temporal domain - each accessible standalone, aggregated by shLowLevel.compareSolutions / shLowLevel.compareSeries.

Single points of access

rep = shLowLevel.compareSolutions(g1, g2, Names = ["CSR", "ITSG"], Plot = true);
rep = shLowLevel.compareSeries({tsCSR, tsGFZ, tsJPL}, Names = ["CSR","GFZ","JPL"]);
rep = g.compare(g2);          % method forms
rep = ts.compare({ts2, ts3});

Both enforce a common basis first: truncation to the smallest nmax, GM/R unification, and (series) epoch matching within a tolerance - unmatched epochs are dropped and reported, never interpolated across gaps. Otherwise the numbers measure processing chains, not solutions.

The metric set

domainmetricfunction
spectraldifference degree amplitude vs both signal spectra; per-degree correlation; agreement crossover degreeshLowLevel.diffSpectrum
spatialarea-weighted (cos φ) bias, RMSD, centered RMSD, pattern correlation, std ratio; Mask= for land/ocean/basinsshLowLevel.spatialStats
temporalNash-Sutcliffe efficiency (punishes bias and amplitude, unlike correlation)shLowLevel.nashSutcliffe
temporalcorrelation with AR(1)-corrected significance (Neff, two-sided p via betainc - base MATLAB)shLowLevel.effectiveCorr
temporaltrend difference ± combined sigma (Kendall AR(1)-corrected fits); annual amplitude ratio and phase lag [days]via compareSeries
errorsχ²/dof of coefficient differences vs combined formal sigmas: tests error realism, a separate question from agreementvia compare*
multi-solutiongeneralized three-cornered hat: individual noise level per solution (N ≥ 3) - the question pairwise metrics cannot answershLowLevel.threeCorneredHat

Visualization

Plot = true renders 4-panel figures: spectra + crossover, degree correlation, difference map and order-RMS of the difference (solutions); comparison series, Taylor diagram, trend-difference spectra and difference series (series). shLowLevel.taylorDiagram(refStd, stds, corrs) stands alone and consumes spatialStats outputs directly.

Validation

All numerics are Python-validated: degree-correlation and Taylor identities to 1e-12, TCH noise recovery within 1% (T = 20000), and the AR(1) correction reduces the false-positive rate of the correlation test from ~30% to ~6% at φ = 0.7.
