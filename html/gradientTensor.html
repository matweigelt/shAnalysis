shLowLevel.shSynthesisGradientTensor

Full gravity gradient tensor in the local north-east-up frame.

Syntax

[G, info] = shLowLevel.shSynthesisGradientTensor(C, S, GM, R, lat, lon, ...
    Height=250e3);      % G.uu G.nn G.ee G.un G.ue G.ne  [1/s^2]

Description

All six independent components at satellite altitude (GOCE diagnostics; 1 Eötvös = 1e-9 s⁻²). Second latitude derivatives of the Legendre functions come from the frozen first-derivative stencil applied twice (legendreALFDeriv, exact; validated vs numerical second differences at the FD floor). Validation invariants: Laplace trace(G) = 0 at 7e-16 of max|G| (built in as info.maxTraceResidual), Guu identical to the 'gravity_gradient_rr' kernel route. East-involving components are NaN at the poles.
