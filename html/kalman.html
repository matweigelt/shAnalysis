Kalman/VAR module — temporally smoothed SH series

The classic chains treat every epoch independently: even the tvANS Wiener filter uses a spatial signal covariance only. The Kalman/VAR module adds the temporal prior of Kurtenbach (2012) and Kvas (2019): a stochastic process model estimated from geophysical model output couples adjacent epochs, so that sparse or noisy per-epoch information (daily solutions, normal equations, gappy series) is stabilized by what the gravity field did the day before.

[ts, rep] = shLowLevel.kalmanChain(obsIn, ModelSeries=tsm, Name=Value)

The idea in four equations

The state x_t is the shIndex-ordered coefficient vector of the short-term residual field. A geophysical model series (AOD1B/GAX, ESA ESM, hydrology), reduced by its climatology, is taken as a realization of the same process; its empirical covariances give the VAR(1) model (Kurtenbach eqs. 3.84–3.88):

B = Σ(1) Σ(0)⁻¹          Q = Σ(0) − B Σ(0) B’
x_t = B x_{t−1} + w,  w ~ N(0, Q)

Observations are fused epoch by epoch by the Kalman filter, followed by the RTS smoother so every estimate uses past and future data. Higher orders VAR(p) (Kvas Sec. 2.4) are estimated by Yule–Walker and run in companion form.

The exactness guarantee. With the stationary initialization x₀⁻ = 0, P₀⁻ = Σ(0) (Kvas eqs. 2.90–2.92), filter + smoother reproduce the joint least-squares adjustment over all epochs to machine precision — unit-tested. The recursion is an exact solver for the block-tridiagonal joint system, never an approximation to it.

The chain stages

Two observation modes

ModeInputUpdate
solutionan shSeries with formal sigmas (or explicit NoiseCov)l = x + v, gain form
neqa folder of SINEX files with NORMAL_EQUATION_MATRIX blocks (fetchITSGSINEX)N x = b, information form — no covariance inversion of the data needed
NEQ backgrounds. Normal equations refer to the provider’s background models. The chain takes them as-is and returns a residual series; restoring GAX / static field is your responsibility, exactly as in the rest of the toolbox.

Typical calls

% smooth a noisy daily-type series with a GAX process prior
tsm = shSeries.fromFolder(gaxFolder).truncate(40);
[ts, rep] = shLowLevel.kalmanChain(tsObs, ModelSeries=tsm, Order=1);

% bridge missing days: prediction-only epochs on a daily grid
grid = (2017 + (0:364)'/365.25);
[ts, rep] = shLowLevel.kalmanChain(tsObs, ModelSeries=tsm, Epochs=grid);
plot(rep.epochs, rep.meanContribution)   % data share per epoch (Kurtenbach 3.3.2)

What to watch

  • Memory. The smoother needs two full (pP×pP) covariances per epoch: n40, p=1, T=365 ≈ 16 GB. Since v3.19, StoreCov="matfile" streams them through a v7.3 MAT on disk with partial I/O — RAM stays flat and the results are identical to the in-RAM path to the last bit (unit-tested). Alternatives: run in windows, lower nmax, or Smoother=false for one-pass NRT-style runs; rep.memGB reports the footprint (disk, in matfile mode).
  • Stationarity. estimateVAR warns when the companion spectral radius reaches 1; an unstable model diverges across long gaps. Shrinkage (Shrink) or covariance conditioning restore stability.
  • Conditioning (v3.18). shLowLevel.buildCondFun builds the CondFun handle implementing the full EWH-domain conditioning of Kvas Sec. 2.4: the covariance is propagated to equivalent water heights on the exact Gauss–Legendre quadrature grid (round trip is the identity at Psi0Km=Inf, unit-tested), cross-region correlations are zeroed by a block mask (his eq. 2.117, e.g. land vs. ocean — ocean-model errors are not correlated with continental hydrology), correlations decay with exp(−ψ/ψ₀) (eq. 2.120), and the result returns to the spectral domain. Both weights are PSD kernels, so the conditioned covariance stays PSD; a rank-deficient empirical Σ(0) from a short model series even becomes strictly positive definite — the Yule–Walker stabilization of his Fig. 2.5. Load Love numbers are required and user-supplied: 
    kn = shLowLevel.readLoveNumbers(lovePath);
cf = shLowLevel.buildCondFun(idx, kn=kn, Psi0Km=1100, Regions=@(la,lo) double(isLand(la,lo)));
model = shLowLevel.estimateVAR(X, Order=1, CondFun=cf);
  • Contribution. rep.meanContribution is the average share of the estimate coming from the data rather than the process model — near 0 the output is the prior wearing your product name.
