shx.mcPropagate

Monte-Carlo uncertainty propagation through arbitrary processing chains.

Syntax

out = shx.mcPropagate(fun, g)                          % from sigmaC/S
out = shx.mcPropagate(fun, g, N=2000, Seed=1)
out = shx.mcPropagate(fun, g, Cov=M, Idx=idx)          % full covariance

Description

Draws coefficient samples consistent with the formal errors of g (independent per-coefficient Gaussians from sigmaC/S, or correlated via a full P×P covariance, e.g. from shx.readSINEX(..., Output="covariance", Index=idx)), pushes each sample through fun(gs), and returns the empirical mean and 1-sigma of any output — filtered grids, basin averages, amplitude maps. The standard cross-check for every analytic sigma in this toolbox.
