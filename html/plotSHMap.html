Visualization

Visualization suite (v2.4)

Maps, basin series, triangle differences, covariance images, spectrum overlays, animations.

shx.plotSHMap / g.map

g.map(-89:89, 0:359, quantity="ewh", kn=kn, Units="m")
shx.plotSHMap(grid, lat, lon, Projection="hammer", CLim=[-0.3 0.3])

Divergent blue-white-red colormap symmetric about zero (built programmatically), robust 98th-percentile limits, coastline overlay from base-MATLAB coastlines, plate-carrée or analytic Hammer equal-area projection — no Mapping Toolbox anywhere.

shx.plotBasinSeries

[avg, out] = shx.basinDeconvolve(b, op);    % b: (P x K) kernel(s)
shx.plotBasinSeries(op.tYears, avg(1,:)', out.sigma(1,:)', Units="cm")

1σ band, grey patches over gaps > 0.3 yr (the 2017–2018 mission gap), AR(1)-corrected trend annotation.

Triangle differences

plotSHCoeffTriangle(gF.C, gF.S, 'RefC', g.C, 'RefS', g.S)

Signed difference on a linear divergent scale — the before/after diagnostic for filter comparisons. Peak stays up, cells stay square (v2.3.1 contract).

shx.plotCovariance

out = shx.readSINEX(f, Output="covariance");
shx.plotCovariance(out.M, out.idx)   % Type="covariance" for log10

Correlation image in shIndex order with (order, C/S) block boundaries — one look shows how block-diagonal the matrix really is.

shx.writeAnimation

shx.writeAnimation(tsF, 'ewh.mp4', quantity="ewh", kn=kn, Units="m")
