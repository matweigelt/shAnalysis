Class

shSeries

A time series of coefficient sets: everything with a time dimension. Mean field, climatology (bias/trend/annual/semi-annual), GAX background restoration, the classic destripe+Gaussian chain applied per epoch, the tvANS time-variable anisotropic Wiener filter, and basin averages with exact deconvolution. Immutable, with provenance in .history.

Construction

ts = shSeries.read("GSM-2_*.gfc");            % pattern; sorted by epoch
ts = shSeries.read(fileList, Truncate=60);    % string array of paths
ts = shSeries(objArray);                      % from shCoefficients array
ts = shSeries(Cs, Ss=Ss, Epochs=ep);          % raw (nmax+1)x(nmax+1)xT stacks

Methods

GroupMethods
Accessat(k) → shCoefficients of epoch k
Statisticsmean(Omitnan=true) → mean field (sigmas = standard error of the mean); ts - ts.mean → anomaly series
Climatology[clim, resid] = climatology(Robust=false, T0=mean(epochs), Periods=[], Weights=[]) → shClimatology + residual series (≥ 6+2K epochs). v2.1: Periods adds extra periodic terms [yr], e.g. the GRACE tidal alias periods [161/365.25, 3.66, 7.48] (S2, K2, K1); Weights takes per-epoch weights (e.g. 1./info.vce). The climatology carries per-coefficient 1-sigma significance.
GAXrestore(gaxSeries, Tolerance=0.05, AllowMissing=false) — epoch-matched add-back of GAA/GAB/GAC/GAD background series; productType becomes e.g. "GSM+GAD"
Classic chaindestripe(...), gaussian(radiusKm), truncate(nmax) — per-epoch, stack-vectorized
Optimal chain[tsF, op, info] = filter("tvANS", ..., Blocks="auto") — see tvANS filter. v2.1: block-diagonal fast path (identical results, tractable to Lmax ~ 120); the output carries posterior 1-sigma stacks tsF.sigmaCs/sigmaSs per coefficient and month.
BasinsbasinAverage(B) naive; basinAverage(B, Deconvolve=true, Op=op, Ridge=0) exact, unbiased. v2.1: out.sigma (K×T) is the propagated 1-sigma noise uncertainty of the deconvolved averages.
Gap handling v2.1dropNaN() removes epochs with any non-finite coefficient (the GRACE↔GRACE-FO gap); select(mask | indices | [tMin tMax]) subsets by mask, index list, or year range. The remaining irregular sampling is handled natively by climatology and the filter.
Arithmeticts - g (field from every epoch) or tsA - tsB (epoch-matched)

Example: full GRACE-FO chain

ts  = shSeries.read("GSM-2_*.gfc", Truncate=60);
ts  = ts.restore(shSeries.read("GAD-2_*.gfc", Truncate=60));  % ocean bottom pressure back
dts = ts - ts.mean;
[clim, resid] = dts.climatology(Robust=true);
[tsF, op] = dts.filter("tvANS", SignalMode="inhomogeneous");
avg = tsF.basinAverage(B, Deconvolve=true, Op=op);            % K x T, unbiased
NaN-containing stacks are accepted for mean(Omitnan=true) but rejected loudly (shSeries:nanInSeries) by the filtering chains — fill or drop months first (ts.dropNaN() since v2.1).
